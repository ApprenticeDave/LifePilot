## Notes on verification and scope

- The research is intentionally bounded to public, accessible guidance material rather than a broad literature review.
- The sources are consistent in one important way: they favor simple, explicit decision-making aids and do not recommend hidden ranking systems for general users.
- The research does not establish a one-size-fits-all task-priority model and should be treated as product guidance, not a proven productivity law.
