# S-029 — Privacy Policy

- Publisher: Ollama.
- URL: https://ollama.com/privacy
- Accessed: 2026-10-18.
- Evidence type: first-party privacy policy.

Ollama says local prompts and model interactions are not collected or
transmitted by Ollama. For cloud-hosted models, it says prompts and responses
are processed transiently to provide the service and are not stored beyond
the time required to fulfill the request; it also says this content is not
used to train models. The policy separately describes account and limited
device or usage metadata collection.

These are provider policy statements, not a third-party audit or a guarantee
of local processing. Requests sent to cloud models leave the user's machine.
Review the current policy, terms, and applicable account terms before sending
personal data.

No content was submitted to Ollama.
