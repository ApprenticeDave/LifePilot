# S-002: My Day

URL: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/todo/my-day-and-suggestions

Publisher: Microsoft Support. Access: 2026-10-18 research session.
Locator: article paragraphs on adding tasks, suggestions and nightly reset.

Original summary: people add tasks directly or from other lists and can open
suggestions to choose items. The daily list resets nightly; incomplete items
remain in Tasks and can appear in subsequent suggestions.
Supports F-02, a user-curated daily focus example.

Caveat: describes one product, not evidence that a reset or empty daily list
works best for everyone. Does not substantiate privacy or clinical claims.
