# S-003: Collecting personal information

- Publisher: GOV.UK / Government Digital Service.
- URL: https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/design/collecting-personal-information-from-users
- Access: research session dated 2026-10-17; see [register](../sources.md) for date caveat.
- Locators: "Do not collect information you do not need"; "Do not store
  information any longer than you need to"; "Tell users what information you're
  collecting and what you'll do with it".
- Original summary: unnecessary questions make services harder and increase
  collection. Explain information use plainly; sometimes a derived answer
  meets the purpose without retaining the underlying document.
- Supports: F-03 and F-05; field-purpose explanations and deferring unrelated records.
- Caveat: UK public-service context, not a legal-basis decision for this product.
  This does not justify silently inferring or storing sensitive profile attributes.
