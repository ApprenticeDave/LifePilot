# S-007: Sensitive inferences

- Publisher: Information Commissioner's Office.
- URL: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/special-category-data/what-is-special-category-data/
- Access: research session dated 2026-10-17; see [register](../sources.md) for date caveat.
- Locator: "What about inferences?" only; partial-page retrieval.
- Original summary: under the ICO's framework, deliberately inferring sensitive
  traits or treating a person differently on that inferred basis can constitute
  special-category processing, regardless of confidence in the inference.
- Supports: F-07; ask about useful support rather than infer a diagnosis from
  selected difficulties.
- Caveats: the opening list of categories was not retrieved. This note does not
  reproduce it or claim a complete legal assessment or full-page review.
