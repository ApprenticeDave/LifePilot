# S-004: Data minimisation

- Publisher: Information Commissioner's Office.
- URL: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-protection-principles/a-guide-to-the-data-protection-principles/data-minimisation/
- Access: research session dated 2026-10-17; see [register](../sources.md) for date caveat.
- Locators: "At a glance"; "How do we decide what is adequate, relevant and
  limited?"; "When could we be processing inadequate personal data?".
- Original summary: collection should be sufficient for its stated purpose,
  connected to that purpose and not excessive. Collecting too little can also
  leave an inadequate basis for the intended use. Review necessity over time.
- Supports: F-03; gather useful basics, not zero context and not a speculative dossier.
- Caveat: this is UK regulatory guidance. Product jurisdiction, intended
  processing and applicable obligations have not been established.
